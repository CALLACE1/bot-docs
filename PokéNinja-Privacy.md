# 🔒 PokéNinja Official Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 6, 2026  
**Bot Name:** PokéNinja ("the Bot")  
**Developer/Owner:** CALL_ACE

This Privacy Policy describes how **PokéNinja** collects, uses, and safeguards information when you invite, configure, or use the Bot in your Discord server. By using the Bot, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
We respect your privacy and strictly adhere to collecting only the minimum data required for the Bot to function:
* **Server (Guild) Data:** Discord Server IDs, channel configurations, role configurations, and active license/subscription status (stored to verify access and run features like mass panels).
* **User Data:** Discord User IDs when interacting directly with bot commands, buttons, dropdown menus, or reservation panels.
* **Command & Interaction Inputs:** Text, numbers, or parameters provided by users while executing commands (e.g., custom embed settings or reservation notes).

*What we do **not** collect:* We do **not** read private direct messages (DMs), track personal web browsing outside of Discord, or store sensitive personal information like real names, phone numbers, or credit card details.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Data
The limited data we collect is used exclusively for the following operational purposes:
* **Licensing & Access Control:** Verifying whether a server has an active subscription key to permit bot usage.
* **Core Functionality:** Operating reservation dashboards, tracking active slots, generating embeds, and managing server-specific configurations.
* **Security & Anti-Abuse:** Preventing system exploitation, managing permissions, and ensuring compliance with our Terms of Service.

---

### 3. Data Storage & Security
* **Secure Storage:** Server configurations and license data are stored securely in local database files (`licenses.json`) managed exclusively by the developer.
* **No Third-Party Sharing:** We do not sell, trade, rent, or transfer your server or user data to any external third parties or advertisers.
* **Access Limitation:** Only the bot application and the developer have access to the backend storage files for maintenance and licensing management.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services
* The Bot operates on the **Discord API** platform. Any data processed through Discord (such as messages, guild structures, and user profiles) is also subject to Discord’s Privacy Policy.

---

### 5. Data Retention & Deletion
* **Server Removal:** If you remove ("kick") the Bot from your Discord server, active operational triggers cease immediately.
* **Data Purge Requests:** Server owners can request a complete deletion of their server's license and configuration history from our database by contacting the developer directly in our support channels.

---

### 6. Children's Privacy
* The Bot is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from anyone under the age of 13 in compliance with Discord’s Terms of Service.

---

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
* We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this document. Continued use of the Bot constitutes acceptance of those changes.
