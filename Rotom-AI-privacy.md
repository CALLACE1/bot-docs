# Privacy Policy for RotomAI

**Effective Date:** September 30, 2026  
**Developer/Contact:** Ace (Support Server: https://discord.gg/z7nsmDfaz)

### 1. Introduction
Welcome to our Privacy Policy. This policy describes how our Discord AI bot collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you interact with the bot across Discord servers or Direct Messages. By using the bot, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

### 2. Information We Collect
To provide a functional and personalized AI experience, we collect and store limited types of data:
* **Discord User IDs:** Used strictly to track user preferences, command limits, and active VIP subscription statuses.
* **Discord Guild (Server) IDs:** Used to save custom server configurations, such as custom AI personalities and server-specific rules.
* **License & Premium Data:** If you redeem a VIP license key, we store your user ID alongside your subscription expiration date in a secure database (`premium.json`). We do **not** store payment card details, as all transactions or key distributions are managed securely by the developer.
* **Chat History (Temporary RAM Storage):** Recent conversation turns are kept temporarily in the bot's active memory (RAM) to provide conversational context. This data is session-based and is not permanently logged into a long-term user database.

### 3. How We Use Your Information
The data we collect is used exclusively for the following purposes:
* Delivering core AI responses and managing conversation memory depth (tiered between Free and VIP users).
* Verifying and maintaining VIP subscription tiers and feature access.

### 4. Third-Party Services
Our bot integrates with third-party artificial intelligence providers (specifically Google Gemini APIs) to generate text responses. When you chat with the bot, your message content is transmitted to the AI provider for processing. We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to any other third parties.

### 5. Data Security & Storage
All configuration files and subscription records are stored locally within secured environment controls managed by the developer.

### 6. User Rights & Data Deletion
You have the right to request the removal of your data at any time. If you wish to clear your data, revoke your subscription status, or have your user identifiers removed, contact the developer directly via our official support server.

### 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted here with an updated effective date.
